How it works
Four steps from hypothesis to validated finding.
1

Design your study

Define conditions, questions, and stimulus text. Choose a template or build from scratch. Set your target persona count and calibration settings.

2

Run calibration

The platform calibrates LLM temperature against a reference effect size from published human data — so your AI responses match the expected human variance.

3

Execute the study

Independent AI personas are assigned to conditions and respond to your survey. Each call is isolated with no shared context between respondents.

4

Analyze & export

Statistical tests run automatically. Review per-question findings, replication status, and export a full report with methodology disclosure.
